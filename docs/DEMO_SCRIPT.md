# Demo video guide (90 seconds) — English

> Show the **working** localhost dashboard while speaking. Keep the yellow/orange "demo" badges visible so you do not claim actual sensor certification.

**0:00–0:15 — Problem**

“Water scarcity affects farmers everywhere—from Djerba in Tunisia to Morocco, Spain, California and India. Awareness campaigns tell farmers to save water. But what if water conservation could earn them something useful?”

**0:15–0:27 — The concept**

“AgroToken is a Bitcoin-inspired rewards system for agriculture. Instead of proof of computing work, we introduce Proof of Water Savings. Verified conservation could one day earn farmers useful digital rewards.”

**0:27–0:50 — Show the working farm model**

“Here’s an olive farm in Djerba. We calculate its irrigation needs from the area, crop coefficient, growing stage, rainfall and FAO-style evapotranspiration. We can pull weather from Open-Meteo. This gives a scientific planning estimate, not a guess from a chatbot.”

**0:50–1:08 — Show mint + wallet**

“For this demo I enter a *simulated* water-meter reading. The system screens against unsafe under-irrigation and an illustrative reference. If eligible, it mints demo AGT and records a transaction. The wallet balance changes immediately.”

**1:08–1:22 — Marketplace + ledger**

“The farmer can spend AGT on agricultural supplies or send tokens to peers. Every local transaction is linked by a SHA-256 hash. If one record is modified, our integrity check detects it.”

**1:22–1:30 — End**

“This is the prototype. Real sensors, validation and agricultural partners come next. AgroToken’s vision is simple: reward every farmer who saves water responsibly.”

## Recording tips

- Record at 1280×720 or 1920×1080 with browser at 100% zoom.
- The app is pre-seeded for the demo; show `/` then My Farms, Water Calculator, Proof of Savings, Wallet, Marketplace, Ledger, AgroAI.
- Do not say "impossible to fake" or "water-backed currency"; a local hash cannot prove real-world water savings.
- Meta Muse integration is real *only* if the API key works. Without a key, correctly show the **LOCAL DEMO** badge.
